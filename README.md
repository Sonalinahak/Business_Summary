# Sales Performance Dashboard | Tableau
### 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing sales performance using Tableau to provide actionable business insights. The dashboard helps stakeholders understand overall revenue trends, regional performance, shipment volume, and top-performing sales representatives to support data-driven decision-making.

### 🎯 Objectives
• Analyze overall sales revenue and shipment activity
• Identify top-performing countries and salespersons
• Provide a high-level business summary for management review
• Enable quick insights through interactive visualizations

### 📂 Dataset
File: sample-data-10mins.xlsx
Description:
•Sales amount
• Country-wise sales data
• Shipment count and boxes shipped
• Salesperson performance metrics

📊 Dashboard Highlights
Business Summary KPIs:
• Total Revenue: ₹6.18M
• Boxes Shipped: 177,007
• Shipment Count: 1,094

Sales by Country
• Australia, UK, India, and USA are the top revenue contributors
• Canada and New Zealand show potential growth opportunities

Top 10 Salespersons
• Identifies highest revenue-generating sales representatives
• Enables performance benchmarking across the sales team

### 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
• Tableau – Data visualization & dashboard development
• Microsoft Excel – Data cleaning and preprocessing

### 📈 Key Business Insights
• Sales are well-distributed across multiple countries, reducing dependency on a single market
• Strong shipment volume indicates efficient distribution operations
• Top salesperson analysis supports incentive planning and sales strategy optimization
